## Description (English)

**Zawias** is a ring-city hanging in the crack between the planes. Any door can become a gate if you carry the
right key, and belief has real weight here.

Someone has stolen the **Key Without a Door** from Mirela the keysmith. Talk, sneak or fight your way through the
Rusty Market to the Unravelers, a faction convinced that everything falls apart, and that this is a good thing.

**What's in this vertical slice**
- Four classes (Fighter, Rogue, Wizard, Cleric), levels 1–3, on the D&D 2024 rules (SRD 5.2)
- Turn-based grid combat: action economy, opportunity attacks, Weapon Mastery, Sneak Attack, concentration, death saves
- Dialogue with skill checks, one quest with three routes and two endings
- Painted isometric backdrop with 3D characters that walk behind buildings and cast real shadows
- Level-up with choices, portraits, music, English and Polish, two difficulty levels

Windows and Linux, OpenGL 3.3.

**Controls**: left click to move, talk and attack; right click cancels; WASD/arrows pan, wheel zooms;
Space ends your turn; F5/F9 save and load.

The Windows build is not code-signed: if SmartScreen appears, choose *More info → Run anyway*.

Source code and the asset pipeline: https://github.com/senssei/zawias

## Opis (polski)

**Zawias** to miasto-pierścień zawieszone w szczelinie między planami. Każde drzwi mogą stać się bramą, jeśli masz
właściwy klucz, a wiara ma tu prawdziwą wagę.

Ktoś ukradł kluczarce Mireli **Klucz bez drzwi**. Przejdź przez Rdzawy Targ słowem, cieniem albo ostrzem
i dotrzyj do Rozplataczy, frakcji przekonanej, że wszystko się rozpada, i że to dobrze.

Cztery klasy na zasadach D&D 2024 (SRD 5.2), turowa walka na siatce, dialogi z testami umiejętności, jeden quest
z trzema drogami i dwoma zakończeniami, malowane izometryczne tło z postaciami 3D. Gra po polsku i po angielsku.
